By Guil Pires , Jennifer Prince , Jose Camacho , Ken Kurzweil , Phanindra Chunduru Background In a previous post, we introduced Data Bridge , a unified management plane for batch Data Movement at Netflix. Historically, several bespoke Data Movement connectors were developed across different engineering organizations to fulfill their specific requirements. Over the last few years, the Data Movement team has started centralizing these offerings through an abstraction that provides a catalog of connectors, along with simple UI and APIs to initiate Data Movement jobs.
