Netflix Conductor : The Next Chapter by Aravindan Ramkumar on behalf of the Conductor team Conductor is the workflow orchestration engine Netflix uses to stitch micro-services into reliable, observable business processes. If you’ve followed Conductor from the outside, the last thing you heard was probably the note on its GitHub repository : Netflix discontinued maintenance of Conductor OSS to refocus its resources on the internal fork. That announcement marked the end of the open-source chapter, and from the outside it may have looked like the end of the story.
